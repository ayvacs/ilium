ilium (/ ˈɪliəm /)

is the widest and largest region of the hipbone of most vertebrates; it is also a free and open archive of ancient texts

what? ilium is best described as a catalogue where you can access, download, and cite english translations of various ancient texts for free. our goal is to centralize these texts, in their most basic form, onto a single catalogue that anyone can access from anywhere, free of charge.
who? ilium was created for people, by people.
where? ilium is easily accessible through any web-browser on any computer or mobile device.
why? ilium was created because it is difficult to find accurate and recent translations of ancient texts, due to various intertwined issues like copyrights, profits, and general capitalism. as a result of this issue, independent researchers and hobbyists find it difficult to access these works if they aren't willing to fork over money to institutions who have not even a trace relation to the original authors. the only true solution to this issue is a free, open source database of texts in their most basic form.
how... can i get involved? you can get involved on our github page. is this site coded? using a static site generator called 11ty and a hosting service called github pages which ensures (in fact, requires) that all of our content stay openly available.
